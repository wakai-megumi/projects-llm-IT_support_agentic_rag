## 3. Password and Account Security Policy

All employees must protect their company accounts and credentials.

### 3.1 Password Requirements

- Use a long, unique password or passphrase for each work account.
- Follow the organization's identity-management system when creating or changing passwords.
- Never share passwords with colleagues, managers, or support personnel.
- Do not store passwords in plain-text files, source code, or shared documents.
- Use an organization-approved password manager when available.
- Enable multifactor authentication (MFA) whenever required.

### 3.2 Forgotten Password

If an employee forgets their password:

1. Open the organization's official sign-in page.
2. Select **Forgot Password** if the option is available.
3. Complete the approved identity-verification process.
4. Follow the password-reset instructions.
5. Sign in again and verify that the account works.

If self-service recovery is unavailable or fails, contact IT Support. Never send your existing password to the support team.

### 3.3 Account Locked

An account may become locked after multiple unsuccessful sign-in attempts or when suspicious activity is detected.

Recommended steps:

1. Stop repeated login attempts.
2. Verify that the username and organization domain are correct.
3. Check whether Caps Lock or the keyboard layout is affecting password entry.
4. Wait for the configured lockout period if applicable.
5. Use the approved account-recovery process.
6. Contact IT Support if the account remains locked.

IT Support may need to verify the user's identity before restoring access.

## 4. Multifactor Authentication (MFA)

MFA adds an additional verification step to the normal sign-in process.

If MFA is not working:

1. Confirm that the registered authenticator device is available.
2. Check the device's internet connectivity if the authentication method requires it.
3. Verify the device's date and time settings.
4. Check whether the authentication request has expired.
5. Use an approved backup method if one has been configured.
6. Contact IT Support if access cannot be restored.

Never approve an unexpected MFA request. Report repeated or suspicious authentication prompts to the IT security team immediately.

## 5. Company Email Usage

Company email should be used for authorized business communication.

Employees should:

- Use their official company email address for work-related activities.
- Avoid opening unexpected attachments or suspicious links.
- Verify unusual requests for money, credentials, or confidential information.
- Avoid forwarding sensitive company information to personal email accounts.
- Report suspected phishing emails to IT Support or the designated security team.

### Common Email Problems

**Problem: Email is not syncing.**

Recommended actions:

1. Verify that the internet connection is working.
2. Confirm that the correct account is selected.
3. Refresh the mailbox or restart the email application.
4. Check whether the mailbox is full.
5. Try the approved webmail interface, if available.
6. Contact IT Support if synchronization still fails.

**Problem: Email attachments cannot be sent.**

Recommended actions:

1. Check the attachment size.
2. Confirm that the file type is permitted.
3. Verify that the recipient's email address is correct.
4. Use an approved company file-sharing service for large files.
5. Contact IT Support if the problem continues.

**Problem: An email may be phishing.**

Do not click links, open attachments, reply, or provide credentials. Use the organization's approved phishing-reporting process. If you have already entered credentials on a suspicious website, contact IT Support or Security immediately and follow their account-recovery instructions.

## 6. VPN and Remote Access Policy

A Virtual Private Network (VPN) provides an approved secure connection to company resources when working remotely.

Employees must use only organization-approved VPN software and access methods.

### VPN Connection Requirements

Before connecting, verify that:

- The device has a working internet connection.
- The approved VPN client is installed.
- The correct organization-provided server or gateway is selected.
- The username and authentication method are correct.
- MFA is completed when required.

### VPN Security

- Do not share VPN credentials.
- Do not install unapproved VPN applications for accessing company systems.
- Disconnect from the VPN when it is no longer needed, according to company policy.
- Report unexpected VPN authentication requests or unusual connection activity.

## 7. Company Laptop and Hardware Usage

Employees are responsible for taking reasonable care of company-issued equipment.

### Laptop Maintenance

- Keep the operating system and approved security software updated.
- Restart the computer when required to complete updates.
- Avoid installing unapproved applications.
- Keep sufficient free disk space.
- Do not disable antivirus or endpoint protection.
- Lock the screen when leaving the workstation unattended.

### Lost or Stolen Equipment

Immediately report a lost or stolen company laptop, mobile device, or storage device to IT Support and the appropriate security contact.

Provide the device type, approximate time of loss, and relevant incident details. Do not attempt to recover a stolen device by confronting a suspected thief.

## 8. Software Installation and Updates

Employees must install software only through approved organizational channels.

To request software installation:

1. Identify the software and its business purpose.
2. Submit a request through the approved IT support portal.
3. Include the application name, version if known, and required deadline.
4. Wait for approval if administrative authorization is required.
5. Follow the installation instructions supplied by IT Support.

Users should not bypass administrator restrictions or install pirated or unauthorized software.

## 9. Wi-Fi and Network Access

Employees must connect to the approved corporate network using the credentials and configuration provided by IT.

For connectivity problems:

1. Confirm that Wi-Fi is enabled.
2. Verify that airplane mode is disabled.
3. Confirm that the correct network is selected.
4. Check whether other devices can connect.
5. Disconnect and reconnect to the approved network.
6. Restart the device if appropriate.
7. Contact IT Support if the issue persists.

Do not share corporate Wi-Fi credentials with unauthorized individuals.

## 10. Data Protection and Confidentiality

Employees must protect company information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and loss.

- Store work files in approved company storage locations.
- Share documents only with authorized recipients.
- Follow data-classification and retention requirements.
- Avoid transferring confidential data to personal cloud services.
- Report accidental disclosure or suspected data loss immediately.
- Never upload company secrets, credentials, or sensitive records to unapproved AI tools.

## 11. IT Support Ticket Guidelines

When submitting a support ticket, include:

- Your name or approved employee identifier.
- The affected device or application.
- A concise description of the problem.
- The exact error message, if available.
- When the issue started.
- The troubleshooting steps already attempted.
- The business impact and urgency.

Do not include passwords, authentication codes, private keys, or other secrets in tickets.

## 12. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I reset my company password?**

A: Use the official password-reset page and complete identity verification. If recovery fails, contact IT Support.

**Q: Who should I contact if my account is locked?**

A: Contact the IT Support Team through the approved support portal or support email.

**Q: Can I install any software on my company laptop?**

A: No. Use approved software and follow the organization's software-request and approval process.

**Q: What should I do if I receive a suspicious email?**

A: Do not interact with the email. Report it through the approved security-reporting process.

**Q: How do I get VPN access?**

A: Request access through the IT support portal if required, install the approved VPN client, and follow the organization's connection instructions.

**Q: Can I share my company password with IT Support?**

A: No. Support staff should use approved identity-verification and account-recovery procedures rather than asking you to disclose your password.

**Q: What information should I provide when reporting an IT issue?**

A: Describe the affected system, the error message, when the issue began, the troubleshooting already attempted, and the impact on your work.

## 13. Escalation Guidelines

Contact the appropriate IT or security team immediately when an issue involves:

- Suspected account compromise.
- Lost or stolen company devices.
- Suspected malware or ransomware.
- Unauthorized disclosure of confidential information.
- Unexpected MFA prompts.
- A major outage affecting multiple users.

Do not delay reporting a suspected security incident while attempting extensive troubleshooting.
